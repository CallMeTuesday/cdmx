Choza closes at 9

Choza is walk-in only, no reservations, not listed on Google, and open Saturday, Sunday and Monday only, roughly 3–9pm. Monterrey 194, next door to Hank's Pizza — no sign.

Monday is the Lunes Listening Lounge: three records played end to end with guest selectors. That's the night you want, but if Alice finishes at 7–8 you get under an hour and there's usually a line.

Fix: go alone around 5:30, wait it out in the downstairs listening room (which is the good part), eat, and have Alice join at 8. Or take it on Saturday instead and lose the listening lounge.

Verify on Instagram @choza_cdmx before you go — hours move.
